How it works

  1. Chart source. We pull the current US Hot 100 chart and map each title to Spotify.
  2. Artist slots. Every 5th track can feature an approved submission from independent artists.
  3. Review. Every submission is reviewed manually. Inclusion is never guaranteed.
  4. Weekly refresh. Updates every Thursday. The playlist is fully rebuilt — old tracks are replaced.
  5. Your part. Save the playlist and listen to several tracks at least once a month so the community stays active.
