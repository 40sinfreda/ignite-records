How it works

  1. Chart source. Each week we pull Billboard Dance/Pop, Dance/Electronic and Hot 100, then map every title to Spotify.
  2. Artist slots. Every 5th track can feature an approved submission from an independent artist.
  3. Review. Every submission is reviewed manually. Inclusion is never guaranteed.
  4. Weekly refresh. The playlist is rebuilt every Wednesday and stays at 100 songs.
  5. Your part. Save the playlist and listen to several tracks at least once a month so the community stays active.
